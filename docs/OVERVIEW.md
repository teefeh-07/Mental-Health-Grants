# Mental Health Grants DAO

The Mental Health Grants DAO is a decentralized platform built on the Stacks blockchain to facilitate transparent, community-driven funding for mental health initiatives.

## Mission
Our mission is to empower individuals and organizations to propose and fund projects that improve mental health accessibility and support systems worldwide.

## Technical Stack
- **Smart Contracts:** Clarity 4.0 (Epoch 3.3)
- **Frontend:** React with TypeScript and Vite
- **Wallet Communication:** Stacks Connect & @stacks/transactions
- **Asset Management:** WalletConnect
- **Hooks & Events:** @hirosystems/chainhooks-client
- **Design:** Modern Glassmorphism with Vanilla CSS
